## 视频生成要求 (15秒)

### main (15秒视频)
```
15-second cinematic video, sustained shot under a Chinese subway canopy at night in heavy rain, Wang Yuan performing CPR with steady rhythm at approximately 110 bpm, sweat mixing with rain on his face, bystanders shifting uncomfortably in the background, the phone on speaker nearby, the empty station entrance beyond the rain, claustrophobic sustained tension rather than rapid cuts, desaturated cold blue tone, 16:9, 24fps
```

### 视频目标
- **目标**：第一章的核心高压视频节点
- **画面内容**：
  - 王远脸部近景
  - 手部按压极近景
  - 围观者脚步后退
  - 雨棚外空旷道路
- **运动方式**：快节奏镜头切换，保持 100-120 次/分钟的体感节拍

### 运动约束
- 风格：写实、电影感、轻手持、紧贴身体
- 不做夸张甩镜、玄幻特效、MV 式炫技转场
- CPR、贴片、清场必须符合急救逻辑
- 不要把胸外按压拍成夸张暴力动作，而是标准受力

### CPR 段补充关键词
- chest compression rhythm 100-120 bpm
- wet fabric recoil
- labored breathing
- realistic fatigue
- crowd pressure in background
